WHAT ARE WE DOING HERE? (BEGINNER BRIEFING) You are an aerospace systems engineer at NASA JPL. Your objective is to design, balance, and assemble a flight-ready spacecraft on Launch Complex 39A, and then fly it to its destination!

Complete Creative Freedom: There is NO single rigid design! Any creative combination of components that satisfies physical requirements will succeed:
  • Positive Power: Solar arrays must generate more power than active instruments draw.
  • Thrust-to-Weight (TWR ≥ 1.0): Rockets must produce enough thrust to overcome gravity.
  • Eclipse Energy: Battery packs must store energy to survive orbital night (shadow occultation).
  • Closed Comms: Antennas must downlink all data generated by cameras and spectrometers.
