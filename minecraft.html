📚 NASA Space Apps Challenge Reference Frameworks
1. Design & Develop Science Missions (NASA Systems Engineering)

Incorporates the 4 mission segments: Launch Segment (Booster thrust authority), Space Segment (CubeSat bus & avionics), Ground Segment (Deep Space Network downlink), and User Segment (Payload science score).

2. General Mission Analysis Tool (GMAT)

Simulates trajectory dynamics, orbital insertion thrust requirements, and low Earth orbit gravity constraints ($TWR \ge 1.0$).

3. Concept Maturity Levels (JPL CML 1–4)

Quantifies mission maturity: CML 1 (Concept Feasibility), CML 2 (Trade Studies), CML 3 (Point Design Validated & Flight Readiness Certified).

4. NASA Mission Design Lab (MDL) Trades

Simulates concurrent multidisciplinary trade-offs: Adding cameras increases science return but demands battery capacity, power generation, mass, and high-gain antenna bandwidth.

5. CubeSat 101 Standard (NASA CSLI)

Employs 1U modular form factors and models the 35-minute orbital shadow occultation (eclipse umbra) where primary solar power drops to 0W.

6. NASA Mission Viz & VR Challenge

Translates abstract numerical orbital mathematics into an intuitive, interactive 3D first-person simulation environment.
